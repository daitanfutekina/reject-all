How to playSaves & privacyCredits

Beat the interface.

Read the command above the website. Click or tap, drag, scroll or hold to do what it says before time runs out. WAIT! means leave the website alone.

Start with four lives. A mistake costs one; a win earns one point. Every six wins, the game speeds up. First encounters include a hint and extra time.

Pause: use the on-screen button, Esc or P. Switching tabs or rotating your phone pauses the run; press Resume when you are ready. Other gameplay needs a mouse or touch screen.

Make room: use the host's fullscreen button on a small screen. On a phone, scrolling inside the simulated page can be part of a task.

Make it yours: Settings includes 1.75× relaxed timers, reduced motion and separate music/effects levels. Sound is optional; commands and results are visible. Audio begins after your first interaction.

Explore the websites for untimed practice, or try a timed practice round from there. Practice does not change your high score or Field Guide.

Same challenge: enter a friend's seed under “Play a specific seed” on the title screen. Embedded games copy the seed; standalone pages can copy a link. The same seed, choices and settings reproduce a run. First-encounter bonuses depend on your own history.

Your progress stays here.

The game saves your best score, Field Guide, encounter history and settings in this browser's local storage. There is no account, cloud save or leaderboard. Another browser or website has its own save.

Clearing site data or choosing “Reset saved progress” in Settings erases that save. Private browsing or blocked storage can make it temporary. If saving is unavailable, the current session still works.

Reject All itself uses no analytics, tracking cookies, advertising SDKs or remote AI calls. Artwork and synthesized audio run locally in the page. The pop-up ads, purchases, permission requests and downloads inside the fake websites are game props.

Your hosting site, including itch.io, handles its own site data under its own policies. Optional Field Guide source links open external websites only when you choose them.

Made to say no.

Independently directed, with code, writing and original artwork made using AI tools under human direction. The game does not generate content or send prompts while you play.

Music: Panic Button, an original score composed in code and synthesized in the browser, with original procedural sound effects. No sampled recordings, voice service or external audio library. Hear it in the Sound Room.

Visuals: original generated product, food, travel and entertainment images; original interface graphics and icons; system fonts. The simulated website identities are fictional. Some clickbait headlines refer to real popular culture, including a Hogwarts house quiz, with original graphics.

Field Guide: entries link to their underlying consumer-protection and privacy sources. Explanations are simplified for play and are not legal advice.
